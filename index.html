Sources, assumptions and calculation audit

Threshold history: 1 January 2016–31 December 2025. Each town has 3,653 valid daily values. P95/P99 use linear interpolation (PERCENTILE.INC). Comparisons use full precision.

GloFAS v4 via Open-Meteo supplies modelled flow on a roughly 5 km grid, not direct gauge readings. OPW gauge locations, neighbouring-cell comparisons and published flow references informed selection. These checks do not establish exact channel matching or local calibration. Coastal/tidal flooding and individual-home predictions are outside scope.

Orange/red rain warnings overlapping the next 48 hours raise one level, capped at Barriers. Yellow does not raise a level. Athlone also checks Roscommon; Carrick-on-Shannon also checks Roscommon. No barriers or no eligible crew means all Standby measures and escalation, while the required Barriers level remains visible.

Allocation respects descending percentage exceedance over P99, then higher peak. It considers feasible assignments across areas before choosing; an otherwise exact tie uses alphabetical town order. Travel-time data is not supplied. Notes are operational facts; unfamiliar restrictions require Duty Officer review.
